# Unit Converter — Technical Background

## What Is a Unit of Measurement?

A unit of measurement is a definite magnitude of a quantity, defined and adopted by convention or by law, that is used as a standard for measurement. Scientific and engineering work depends on accurate unit conversions to avoid errors.

## Measurement Systems

### SI (International System of Units)
The modern metric system, used in science and most of the world:
- Length: meter (m)
- Mass: kilogram (kg)
- Temperature: Kelvin (K), Celsius (°C)
- Time: second (s)

### Imperial / US Customary
Still used in the United States and a few other countries:
- Length: inch (in), foot (ft), yard (yd), mile (mi)
- Mass: ounce (oz), pound (lb), ton
- Temperature: Fahrenheit (°F)

## Category Overview

### Length
`1 mile = 1.60934 km`
`1 inch = 2.54 cm`
`1 foot = 30.48 cm`

### Mass / Weight
`1 pound = 453.592 grams`
`1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds`

### Temperature
- Celsius to Fahrenheit: `°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32`
- Fahrenheit to Celsius: `°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9`
- Celsius to Kelvin: `K = °C + 273.15`

### Area
`1 acre = 4,047 m²`
`1 hectare = 10,000 m²`

### Volume
`1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters`
`1 liter = 1,000 cm³`

### Speed
`1 mph = 1.60934 km/h`
`1 knot = 1.852 km/h`

## Famous Unit Conversion Failures

The **Mars Climate Orbiter** (1999) was lost because one team used metric units while another used imperial units, causing the spacecraft to enter the atmosphere at the wrong angle.
