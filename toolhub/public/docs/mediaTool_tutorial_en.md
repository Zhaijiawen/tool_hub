# Media Processing — Usage Tutorial

The three tabs at the top correspond to the three features.

## Video transcode

1. In the **Transcode** tab, upload a video (MP4 / MOV / WebM / MKV / AVI, etc.).
2. Pick the output format: **MP4 (H.264)** or **WebM (VP8)**.
3. Pick quality (CRF): left = higher quality/larger, right = smaller/lower quality.
4. If you need to shrink it, pick a scale preset.
5. Click **Start transcode**; preview and download when done.

## Audio extraction

1. Switch to the **Audio** tab, upload a video or audio file.
2. Pick the output: MP3 / M4A / WAV / OGG / Opus.
3. Click **Extract audio** and download.

## Video to GIF

1. Switch to the **GIF** tab, upload a video.
2. Adjust frame rate (5-30fps) and width (80-800px). Lower fps and width make a lighter GIF.
3. Click **Make GIF** and download.

## Tips

- Video for the web? **WebM** usually saves bandwidth.
- Only want sound, no picture? Use **Audio extraction** with **MP3**.
- GIF suits short looping clips; don't convert long videos to GIF — it gets huge.
