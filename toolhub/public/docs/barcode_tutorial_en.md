# Barcode Scanner · Tutorial

## Scan from an image
1. Open the **Barcode Scanner** tool; it starts on the "Upload image" tab.
2. **Upload an image**: click the dashed box, or drag an image into it (common formats supported).
3. Click **"Scan"**. On success, results appear below — each with symbology type and decoded text.
4. Click **"Copy"** on the right of a result to copy its text.

## Live camera scanning
1. Switch to the **"Camera"** tab.
2. Click **"Start camera"**; the browser asks for camera permission. Allow it to enter live view.
3. Point the barcode at the lens — it decodes automatically and appends to the result list (duplicates are skipped).
4. Click **"Stop"** to turn off the camera; click **"Clear"** to empty the result list.

## FAQ
- **Nothing detected?** The barcode may be blurry, glared, or distorted — use a sharper image or adjust angle and lighting and retry.
- **Camera won't open?** Check that the browser has camera permission and that no other app is using the camera.
- **Want batch?** Scan continuously (results append) or upload several images one by one.
