# Calculator Examples

Here are some real-world things you might actually punch into a calculator.

## Everyday Arithmetic

Nothing fancy, just the stuff you reach for a calculator for:

```
125 + 37 = 162
1000 - 456 = 544
24 × 15 = 360
100 ÷ 8 = 12.5
```

## Percentage Tricks

Percentages trip people up all the time. Here's how to handle the common ones:

```
# What's 20% of 500? Multiply 500 by 20%:
500 × 20% = 100

# Price after a 15% discount on a 1200 item:
1200 × (1 - 15%) = 1200 × 0.85 = 1020
(Tip: (1 - discount%) is faster than calculating the discount and subtracting)

# 75 is what percentage of 300?
75 ÷ 300 × 100 = 25%
```

The percentage button converts the number to its decimal form -- `15%` becomes `0.15`. Multiply that by anything to get that percentage of it.

## Science and Math

```
# Area of a circle with radius 5:
π × 5² = 3.14159 × 25 ≈ 78.54

# Compound interest: 10000 principal, 5% annual, 3 years:
10000 × 1.05³ = 10000 × 1.157625 ≈ 11576.25
(Each year multiplies by 1.05, so 3 years is 1.05³)

# Pythagorean theorem -- hypotenuse of a 3-4-5 triangle:
√(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5
```

## Quick Unit Conversions

You can use the calculator for conversions in a pinch:

```
# Celsius to Fahrenheit (37°C body temp):
(37 × 9 ÷ 5) + 32 = 98.6°F

# Miles to kilometers (100 miles):
100 × 1.60934 = 160.934 km

# Megabytes to bytes (250 MB):
250 × 1000 × 1000 = 250,000,000 bytes
```

Not a replacement for a proper converter, but when you just need a quick number it does the job.
